# ATSH AI cinematic starter art

The catalog uses the user's ATSH AI corporate-presenter illustration as the visual reference for a cohesive expressive, cinematic 2.5D animation style. Image-generated source atlases were split into individual transparent WebP sprites or 16:9 venue backgrounds and bounded to the actual library size constraints (each WebP at most 180 KiB). The six human characters have age- and gender-appropriate provisional Egyptian voice identities; other Egyptian dialect variants remain selectable from the voice library. No claim is made that the temporary synthetic voices are authentic native recordings.

Prompt families: reference-matched formal presenters, same-identity casual clothing and action atlases; Egyptian teenage and child characters; Cairo office, restaurant, school, clinic, shop, home, street, workshop, hospital; cat, dog, car, delivery van, tuk-tuk, open/closed door, office furniture, restaurant furniture and plant. Original design reference belongs to the user; no third-party brand assets were added.

The existing layered compositor uses separate poses, still-background camera framing and audio amplitude-driven speech switching. These are not rigged animation models or phoneme-accurate lip sync. Assets are bundled for preview and one-click import. Existing uploaded assets are not overwritten.
